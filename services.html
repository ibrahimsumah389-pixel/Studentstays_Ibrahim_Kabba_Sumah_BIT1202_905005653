Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything to use StudentStay?

No. Searching, saving and contacting providers is free for students. Property owners can list rooms for free during our launch period.

Are the listings checked?

Yes. Our team reviews each new listing and confirms the owner's phone number before it is published.

How do I pay rent?

You agree payment directly with the provider. Never pay a deposit before you have seen the room and received a written agreement.

What happens to my personal information?

We only share your name, email and phone number with the provider you send a request to. We never sell your data.
